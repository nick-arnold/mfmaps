About

What this is

MF Maps is a foraging platform built cartography-first. The map layers come from the same public datasets professional GIS work runs on — LANDFIRE and FIA TreeMap for species, MTBS for burn history, NHD for hydrography, PAD-US for land ownership, ERA5 for soil moisture and temperature — reprojected, tiled, and served as vector and raster tiles rather than screenshots.

The reference sections cover flora, fauna, fungi, and fish. Mycologists use funga as the parallel term to flora and fauna, and it's the more precise word; the nav says Fungi because that's what people search for.

A word on risk

Probability layers narrow a search. They do not identify anything. Misidentified mushrooms kill people every year, and no map changes that. Verify in the field, with a key, and when in doubt leave it.
